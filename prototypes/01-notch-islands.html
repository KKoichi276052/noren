Switch log

    What to judge

    • Quick toggles. A fast ⌥ tab, let go, flashes the islands for a moment. Compare a show delay of 0 with about 100 ms, the way Cmd-Tab hides itself for quick taps.
    • Bud versus slide. Does budding read as the notch itself growing, and does it still feel good the fiftieth time? Try slow motion to see the shape change.
    • Pins by position. With "Typical", can you reach the terminal without looking (◀ ◀ ◀ from the start, or 3)?
    • Crowding. Switch to "Many". Is the arc still easy to scan with six pins and eight recent apps on screen?
    • Contrast. Try the light wallpaper and the external display. Do black islands still hold their shape?
    • Duplicates. A pinned app that is running appears on both sides: pins stay where they are, and the right side always follows Cmd-Tab order. Does seeing the icon twice bother you?

    Browser frame timing differs from a native panel, so treat the durations as relative. The window list shows titles only, which matches the v1 plan of asking for Accessibility access alone.
