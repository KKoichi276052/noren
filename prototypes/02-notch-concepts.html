Switch log

    What to judge

    • Columns. Does the notch widening into a shelf feel like the hardware itself growing? Is ▲ ▼ inside a column as natural as niri's focus up and down?
    • Axle. Does the wheel feel physical when you hold ▶? Can you still read the window fan while it spins? Try 5 windows to see windows ride the wheel directly.
    • Loupe. Does the selected window read as being pulled out through the notch? With 19 windows, do the tiny menu-bar thumbnails help you, or are they noise?
    • Card flies vs pours from notch. Which one feels faster? Compare both with Instant to see what the motion costs.
    • All at once. Switch between 5 and 11 windows. Is six the right cut-off before grouping by app?
    • Hardware look. Try the light wallpaper and the external display. Does the pure black still read as part of the Mac?

    Thumbnails are drawn placeholders. The real app would capture window images with Screen Recording access and fall back to icon-and-title cards without it. Per-app hotkeys replace pins and are not simulated here. Browser frame timing differs from a native panel, so treat durations as relative.
